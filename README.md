# FoodHub Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project explores and analyzes food order data from **FoodHub**, a food aggregator company operating in New York. The goal is to provide data-driven insights and actionable recommendations to enhance customer experience, optimize delivery logistics, and increase overall business revenue.

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Insights

### 1. Customer Engagement & Ratings
* **Low Rating Submissions:** Approximately **39% of all orders** (736 out of 1898) do not receive a rating from customers.
* **Top Rated:** Among rated orders, the overall sentiment is highly positive with `5.0` being the most frequent rating.

### 2. Operational Logistics
* **Preparation vs. Delivery:** On average, preparing the food takes **27.37 minutes**, while delivering it takes **24.16 minutes**—leading to an average total order-to-delivery turnaround time of **51.53 minutes**.
* **Slow Deliveries:** About **10.5% of orders** take more than **60 minutes** to get delivered from the moment they are placed.
* **Weekday vs. Weekend Delivery:** Weekday delivery times are significantly longer on average (**28.34 mins**) compared to weekend deliveries (**22.47 mins**), likely due to traffic or courier availability.

### 3. Business Demand & Revenue
* **Cuisine Popularity:** The most popular cuisines are **American, Japanese, Italian, and Chinese**.
* **Weekend Dominance:** Order volume during weekends is more than **double** that of weekdays.
* **Top Restaurants:** The top 5 restaurants generating the highest order volume are:
  1. **Shake Shack** (219 orders)
  2. **The Meatball Shop** (132 orders)
  3. **Blue Ribbon Sushi** (119 orders)
  4. **Blue Ribbon Fried Chicken** (96 orders)
  5. **Parm** (68 orders)
* **Revenue Metrics:** Net revenue generated from the company's commission structure (25% on orders > $20; 15% on orders > $5) totals **$5,340.44**.

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

1. **Feedback Gamification:** Introduce small incentives (e.g., loyalty points, $1 discount off the next order) to reduce the 39% unrated order gap and collect richer customer satisfaction data.
2. **Optimize Weekend Staffing:** Since weekend volume is more than double, partner restaurants should increase kitchen staff on weekends to maintain efficient prep times, and FoodHub should run targeted courier shifts during peak weekend hours.
3. **Solve Weekday Traffic Bottlenecks:** Address the higher weekday delivery times (28.3 mins vs 22.4 mins) by optimizing dispatch routing algorithms or offering couriers zone-specific delivery options (e.g., e-bikes for dense grid-lock zones).
4. **Targeted Promotions:** Leverage the 4 highly popular and highly rated restaurants (**Blue Ribbon Fried Chicken, Blue Ribbon Sushi, Shake Shack, and The Meatball Shop**) in promotional campaigns to attract and acquire new customers.
